Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play Age of Fronts?

Select your squads with left click or drag. Right click land to move, right click enemies to attack, and hold Shift to queue waypoints. Recruit units with Q (infantry), W (archers), E (cavalry), T (transports), and B (warships).

Is Age of Fronts free?

Yes, Age of Fronts is completely free to play in your browser with no installation, download, or registration required.
