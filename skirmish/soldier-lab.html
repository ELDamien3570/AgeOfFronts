Statistical sandbox

Unit selection loads authored health and speed percentages, damage, range and reload. Accuracy starts at 100% as an explicit estimate; the game has no authored accuracy field. Changing units resets these statistical inputs.

Expected volley = troop count × damage × accuracy; expected DPS = volley ÷ reload seconds. Assumes every soldier fires once per cycle. Armour, ranks, movement penalties, magazine timing, charge and target bonuses are excluded. Health is the unit's health modifier, not individual HP.
