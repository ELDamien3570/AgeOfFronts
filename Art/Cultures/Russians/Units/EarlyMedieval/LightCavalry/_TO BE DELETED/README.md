# Russian Early Medieval Light Cavalry

Nine solo clips, 54 frames: idle, walk, spear thrust, get hit, get charged, gallop, charge thrust, together fall, and thrown rider. The approved Idle-v1.png is preserved. ImageGen creates whole poses; SourceArt/compose_animations.py uniformly scales and pads whole frames with fixed saddle pivots. Native sources, generated alpha, prompts, hashes, and the superseded thrown-death draft are retained. Attack impact is frame four.
