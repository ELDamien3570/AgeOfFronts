# Russian Bronze Age Bronze Axeman

Single animated actor for user review before forming his five-man unit. Broad bronze axe heads and round timber shields define the silhouette. A hammered bronze helmet, paired bronze pauldrons, stitched reddish-brown leather top, cream fur and pale trousers carry the established stylized Russian identity. Bronze, timber and leather equipment has modest wear and rougher surfaces. This is an imagined ancestral roster, not a historical Slavic army.

Nine six-pose clips cover idle, walk, normal axe chop, light hit, distinct charge impact, charge running, heavy charge chop and two complete deaths. The side death buckles, kneels and falls sideways; the backward death staggers, lifts the feet and settles supine. These are separate authored pose sequences for mixing in the eventual formation. Formation assembly and gameplay integration await actor acceptance.

`animations.json` supplies selected filenames, editable durations, authored body/root pivots, source-frame order and one fixed scale per clip. Frames are not fitted to their changing silhouettes. The run's authored source order groups the right-foot reach, passing and gather before the left-foot reach and gather. Formation tracks will use the running source for separate charge-in and maintain-charge phases, followed by the heavy strike and return to standard ranks.

All source sheets are RGBA 1536x1024 with six 512px cells in a 3x2 grid. `Generation.json` retains exact built-in ImageGen prompts, references, native generated paths and selected/superseded filenames. Original outputs are copied into this folder and alpha is preserved. Earlier drafts remain available; padding corrections redraw the full pose inside its cell rather than crop weapons or limbs.

`Actor_Review.html` reuses the established single-actor review view model. It offers nine clip tabs, frame stepping, repeat/replay, facing rotation, anchors, backdrop selection and 32/48/64px previews. Disable Repeat reactions for review to watch a full death and retain its final frame.

`Validation.json` records source hashes, distinct frame checks, visible edge guards, transparency and registered canvas bounds. Its additional playback-frame checks follow authored source rectangles, including the reordered charge frames. `Browser-Review.json` records browser verification separately from user approval and engine integration. Browser screenshots are review evidence, not native engine or match-runtime certification.

Base civilization art and Stone Age artwork are preserved. No combat, movement, progression, collision, balance or gameplay code is changed.
