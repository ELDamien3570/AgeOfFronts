# Soviet gun-nest rebuild

The user rejected the earlier generic weapon and forward-bag revision and
requested a fresh Soviet machine-gun nest. Icon-v3.png is the new DShK-based
composition; Icon-v4.png refines the overhead camera and is the selected Icon.png.

The Australian War Memorial identifies the foreground weapon in
[photograph P01595.071](https://www.awm.gov.au/collection/C239374) as a Soviet
DShK heavy machine gun. Its [restored DShK photograph P02570.013](https://www.awm.gov.au/collection/C340363)
provides another view. Both primary photographs were visually inspected through
their linked collection images. They were research references, not input images
to the fresh generation call.

The visible silhouette informed the finned barrel, stout muzzle brake, substantial
receiver, left feed housing and paired rear grip treatment. The overhead tripod,
compact front/side sandbag parapet and open rear access are game-art composition.
This is an illustrative external silhouette, not an exact mechanical reconstruction.

Generation.json records both full prompts and the reference URLs. All prior
masters are retained; the v2 extension is recorded as rejected. No other culture
building image was changed.
