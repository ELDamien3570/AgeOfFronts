# Russian Modern building direction

The user chose mixed Soviet-era brick/concrete industry and contemporary
Russian facilities, and requested the full Modern roster. This is an illustrative
game-art family, not a reconstruction of a named real military installation.

The Museum of Moscow's [New Cheryomushki tour](https://mosmuseum.ru/tours/p/new_cheremushki/)
describes the experimental neighborhood associated with standardized mass housing.
The City uses broad concrete apartment roof blocks around a landscaped public
courtyard. The indexed museum description was available; the page itself returned
HTTP 403 during this research. Roof layouts and playground colors are art choices.

The museum's [Industrial Lefortovo exhibition](https://mosmuseum.ru/exhibitions/p/industrial_lefortovo/)
documents Moscow's industrial district and its electrical, cable, machine and
other factories. Its [Elektrozavod feature](https://mosmuseum.ru/news/p/elektrozavod_video/)
describes the large historic industrial complex and its ongoing industrial use.
These support the inherited industrial character: brick-edged work halls, larger
metal roof areas, cable reels, machine goods and orderly cargo yards. They do not
establish the exact plan or roof palette used here.

Warm brick, grey concrete, olive metal roofs and restrained burgundy accents
connect the set. Civilian housing, civilian manufacture, recruitment, equipment
production, resource sites and defenses each lead with a distinct large role cue.
National emblems are unnecessary for these silhouettes.

The live roster is verified against src/skirmish/content/Buildings.ts,
src/skirmish/content/Equipment.ts, src/skirmish/content/Units.ts and
src/skirmish/Protocol.ts. Modern has Vehicle Depot and Arms Factory; Stables,
Blacksmith, Armory and Tower are unavailable in this age. The base Modern Stables
image already depicts a vehicle facility and is retained as Depot comparison art.

The seventeen roles include gun nest, trench, fixed missile defense and MIRV
launch complex. Anti-air gun-nest art exists in the base terrain kit but is not a
distinct current BuildingType, so it is not invented as a new gameplay role here.
The trench is a representative inspection icon. No connected terrain tiles or
weapon animation sheets are replaced by this source-art pass.
