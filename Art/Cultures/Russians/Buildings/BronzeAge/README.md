# Russian civilization: Bronze Age buildings

This draft set evolves the approved Stone Age forest-settlement language with
heavier fitted timber, more organized work areas and bronze equipment. All
images are intended for a directly overhead orthographic view and small-size
role recognition. Keep the existing base art and approved Stone Age set intact.

The user requested stronger age progression: small palisades around the city,
barracks, archery range and stables; a siege tower alongside the ram; three
factory chimneys; and one larger boat at the port. These features belong to the
current selected images. Earlier drafts remain in each role's `SourceArt/` folder.

The four palisades use tightly packed stake top caps from directly above, with
open entrances and minimal contact shadows. Their earlier wall views remain
preserved as version 2; version 3 is the current selected overhead correction.

The regional archaeological references are recorded in `Regional_References.md`.
They guide a few selected cues; the set is a game interpretation, not a faithful
reconstruction or a claim of Bronze Age Russian ethnic identity. Roof materials
and construction details continue the approved art direction.

| Folder | Role | Main visual cue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Barracks | Infantry recruitment | Palisaded drill yard, spear/axe racks and training dummies |
| City | Population and reserve growth | Palisaded household blocks and central well |
| Archery Range | Ranged recruitment | Palisaded shooting lanes and straw targets |
| Stables | Cavalry recruitment and horse production | Palisaded U-shaped paddock and three horses |
| Factory | Trade goods and gold income | Three kiln chimneys, pottery, textiles and finished hides |
| Port | Naval recruitment and maritime trade | Finger piers and one large wooden cargo boat |
| Mine | Resource extraction | Open pit, shaft opening and ore spoil |
| Siege Workshop | Siege equipment and recruitment | Siege tower and ram assembly, wheels and timber stock |
| Tower | Automatic ground defense | Compact square lookout deck |
| Blacksmith | Troop equipment production | Casting hearth, molds, crucibles and bronze pieces |

`Icon.png` is the current selected review image in each role folder.
`SourceArt/` preserves generated masters and earlier iterations.
`Generation.json` records the exact prompts and image references used by the
built-in image generator. `Source_Validation.json` checks the selected PNGs;
`Browser_Validation.json` records local inspector checks and their limitations.

Artwork identity uses the existing building role ID together with the age.
No gameplay, unlocks or runtime culture selection are changed by these drafts.
