# Classical Age regional references

The user chose a Black Sea stone-building influence on October 2, 2026.
Phanagoria supplies the principal architectural cues. This is an art direction
for the game's Russian culture, not a reconstruction or an ethnic attribution
of ancient Black Sea settlements.

## Research and visual use

- [Phanagoria 2019 excavation report, Institute of Archaeology RAS](https://archaeolog.ru/expeditions/expeditions-2019/fanagoriyskaya-ekspeditsiya-2019):
  the early Classical layers include multi-room mudbrick buildings, paved
  streets, a stone drainage cistern and a metalworking hearth. Use orderly
  building blocks, pale paving, a central water feature and a distinct forge.
  The report's sixth-to-eighth-century houses and winery are later material;
  they are not used as Classical-period evidence.
- [Phanagoria archaeological album, Institute of Archaeology RAS, 2015](https://www.archaeolog.ru/media/books_2015/Phanagoriya_alb_2015.pdf):
  the indexed architectural discussion describes tiled residential and public
  roofs, architectural terracotta and metalworking workshops. Use terracotta
  roofs and craft-yard cues. The full PDF could not be opened by the browser
  tool; this cue was checked against its indexed extract.
- [Stone material in buildings of the Phanagoria acropolis, research article, 2022](https://pifk.magtu.ru/%D0%B6%D1%83%D1%80%D0%BD%D0%B0%D0%BB/67-%E2%84%963-77-%2C-2022-%D0%B3/1284-51.html):
  the indexed abstract reports stone foundations of three major buildings and
  substantial building-stone supply in the fifth and fourth centuries BCE.
  Use pale stone footings and horizontal wall caps. The full article was not
  accessible through the browser tool; the abstract is the evidence used.

## Game interpretation

The unified pale limestone palette, full stone military enclosures, specialized
recruitment buildings, kiln chimney count and combined siege yard serve age
progression and role clarity. They are design choices rather than attested
plans from those excavations. Existing role cues continue: two training dummies,
three targets, three horses, multiple factory flues and a single larger boat.
All enclosing walls are drawn as horizontal top surfaces, avoiding visible
vertical wall elevations and isometric presentation.
