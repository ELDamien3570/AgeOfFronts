# Russian Bronze Age River Archer

Nine six-frame single-actor animations are available for review: idle, walk, bow shot, get hit, get charged, charge in / maintain, advance then plant and shoot, side death, and backward death. Bronze helmet, pauldrons, fur, leather, wristguards, trousers, boots, wooden bow, bronze-tipped arrow and leather quiver follow the approved idle. No shield. This is the imagined ancestral Russian roster.

`Idle-v1.png` preserves the approved native RGBA design unchanged. `SourceArt/Animation-Generation.json` records the built-in ImageGen prompt set; `Generation.json` includes design and animation provenance. Selected native sheets are preserved in `SourceArt`. `animations.json` supplies the selected frames, durations, loops, release markers and fixed authored roots. `Validation.json` verifies 54 distinct frames, cell borders, registered preview bounds and preservation of the idle.

`SourceArt/compose_animations.py` reproduces whole-pose composition with a common 0.80 bake scale. `SourceArt/Composition.json` records source rectangles, roots and offsets.
