# Russian Bronze Age Light Cavalry

Nine six-frame mounted spearman animations for review: idle, walk, spear thrust, hit, charged impact, gallop, charge thrust, together fall, and thrown rider. Hammered bronze helmet, paired pauldrons and bracers, reddish-brown leather top, cream fur collar, pale trousers and boots match the Bronze Spearman. The chestnut horse and leather tack follow the Scout. The approved rider has no shield; its free hand holds the reins.

`Idle-v1.png` preserves the native built-in ImageGen RGBA output. `Generation.json` stores the complete prompt and references. `animations.json` registers fixed saddle roots, timings and impact frames at the standard solo preview footprint. `Validation.json` records all 54 distinct frames, transparent padding guards, preview bounds and approved-idle preservation. Native atlases and exact generation prompts remain in `SourceArt`; `compose_animations.py` rebuilds the atlases with one common scale and no painted corrections. Attack source order is explicitly recorded. Both initial attack sheets remain archived after spear-direction corrections. Animation acceptance is pending user visual review.

Base art and previous Russian sources remain preserved. The gallery addition is artwork only; no gameplay or formation changes.
