# Russian civilization: Late Medieval buildings

Ten approved building icons continue the Russian culture with a fourteenth-to-
fifteenth-century Novgorod/Pskov influence. Developed timber courtyard buildings,
dark wooden-shingle roofs, limestone defenses and organized working courts
provide a visible advance over the approved Early Medieval set.

| Folder | Role cue |
| --- | --- |
| Barracks | L-shaped barrack roof, two training dummies, armor and iron weapons |
| City | Developed courtyard households, communal hall, well and gathering square |
| Archery Range | Three large targets, long lanes, bows and crossbows |
| Stables | U-shaped roof, three horses, organized feed, water and cavalry tack |
| Factory | Three chimney openings, two kiln tops, ceramics, weaving and furs |
| Port | One broader cargo vessel, aft deckhouse, stepped warehouses and finger piers |
| Mine | Concentric quarry ledges, masonry-lined shaft and overhead capstan |
| Siege Workshop | Counterweight trebuchet, separate covered siege tower and ram |
| Tower | Round limestone parapet deck with crossbow, spears and shields |
| Blacksmith | Square glowing forge, anvil, weapons, armor and iron stock |

Stone enclosures are limited to City, Barracks, Archery Range and Stables.
All ten images preserve the directly overhead camera. Eight version-2 masters
correct roofs, supports, masonry tops, docks, quarry ledges or equipment details;
the siege revision also removes a competing anvil and unintended boundary stones.

`Icon.png` is each selected approved image. `SourceArt/` retains all generated masters
and iterations. `Generation.json` records the exact full initial prompts,
per-asset reference images, built-in image_gen outputs and revision prompts.
`Regional_References.md` separates archaeological cues from game interpretation.
`Source_Validation.json` checks transparency, solid-content containment, master
parity and preservation of all 39 approved earlier-age images.

The inspector uses existing role IDs together with the age. Base art remains
in its original folders. The user approved all ten selected images on October 2, 2026. Runtime
integration and publication remain separate steps.
