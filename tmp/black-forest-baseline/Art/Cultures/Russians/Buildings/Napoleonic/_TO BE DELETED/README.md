# Russian civilization: Napoleonic buildings

Ten approved icons develop the Russian culture with sixteenth–seventeenth-century
Muscovite brick/timber compounds, cream stone trim, muted grey-green roof tops,
organized work yards and gunpowder equipment. Roof palette and layouts are
game-art interpretations; see Regional_References.md for the source boundary.

| Folder | Role cue |
| --- | --- |
| Barracks | Two training dummies, muskets, sabres and broad berdysh poleaxes |
| City | Larger household groups, civic hall, communal well and gathering square |
| Archery Range | Three large targets, separated long lanes and firearm supplies |
| Stables | Four overhead horses, hay/water, saddles, cavalry sabres and pistols |
| Factory | Three chimney openings, dye vats, weaving, ceramics and packed goods |
| Port | One larger cargo vessel, two piers, warehouse roofs and cargo quay |
| Mine | Concentric extraction pit, timber hoist, capstan and ore carts |
| Siege Workshop | Two field guns, shorter howitzer, casting moulds and carriage stock |
| Tower | Square brick gun platform with one cannon and small hipped canopy |
| Armory | Weapon-finishing parts, muskets, sabres, armor and secondary forge |

Armory follows the existing Napoleonic equipment-production role. It does
not reuse the earlier Blacksmith ID. The ranged building keeps its stable
Archery Range category while its supplies follow the firearm-age content.
Only City, Barracks, Archery Range and Stables have town/recruitment enclosures.

Each selected Icon.png matches SourceArt/Icon-v2.png. All twenty generation
masters remain in the building folders. The ten revisions remove camera tilt,
facades, post shafts, pit risers, forward-facing gun mouths or unintended yard
walls while preserving role cues. Generation.json records exact full prompts,
reference images, generated output locations and revision history.

Approved_Art_Baseline.json records all 49 approved earlier-age images before
this set was generated. Source_Validation.json checks their preservation,
selected-master parity, alpha and solid-content containment. Browser_Validation.json
records local inspector checks; Inspection_Review.png and Full_Inspection_Review.png
show the review page and small previews.

The existing culture/age/category inspector consumes declarative artwork data.
