# Russian civilization: Early Medieval buildings

Ten approved building icons use early Rus and East Slavic timber identity, with
Ladoga and Novgorod as regional anchors for the ninth to eleventh centuries.
Notched log framing, weathered grey plank roofs, wooden streets, river cargo
and iron equipment distinguish the set from the approved Classical stone art.
The images keep the directly overhead camera established in previous reviews.

| Folder | Role cue |
| --- | --- |
| Barracks | Framed timber drill court, two training dummies and iron weapons |
| City | Five log households, crossing wooden streets and a central well |
| Archery Range | Three large targets, long firing lanes, bows and arrows |
| Stables | U-shaped plank roof, three horses, hay, tack and a log water trough |
| Factory | Three chimney openings, two pottery kilns, cloth, loom and furs |
| Port | One larger laden river boat, timber piers and a plank-roofed warehouse |
| Mine | Concentric quarry ledges, log-lined shaft, ore and mining tools |
| Siege Workshop | Traction stone thrower, separate covered siege tower and ram |
| Tower | Open timber defensive deck, small canopy, spears and shields |
| Blacksmith | Glowing forge, overhead anvil, bellows, iron bars and weapons |

Enclosure walls appear on City, Barracks, Archery Range and Stables only.
Their horizontal timber caps suggest framed timber-and-earth construction.
Factory version 2 removes an unintended perimeter rail. Port, Mine, Tower and
Blacksmith version 2 flatten roof outlines, supports and equipment details.

The user approved all ten selected images on October 2, 2026. `Approval.json`
records that approval and the SHA256 hash of each selected image.

`Icon.png` is each selected image. `SourceArt/` retains the generated masters
and all iterations. `Generation.json` records the exact prompts, references
and built-in image_gen outputs. `Regional_References.md` distinguishes the
archaeological cues from the gameplay interpretation. `Source_Validation.json`
checks transparency, solid-content containment, master parity and the hashes
of all 29 approved earlier-age images.

The culture manifest uses the existing building IDs together with the age.
The base artwork and approved Stone, Bronze and Classical sets remain intact.
This set has source-art approval; runtime integration and publication remain
separate steps.
