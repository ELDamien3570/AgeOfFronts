# Russian Early Medieval trader

One Rus merchant in a green wool tunic with one chestnut horse and a fur-laden two-wheel cart. Built-in imagegen master and full prompt are retained in SourceArt and Generation-Manifest.json. Creation and animation were authorized together; visual acceptance is pending.

Idle and Travel each have 10 frames in 2560 by 1024 transparent sheets, with centered 512 px frames. The corrected caravan rig drives six articulated legs, planted stance, lifted recovery, rolling tread and shared ground speed. The cargo, reins and shafts remain fixed in the body assembly. No gameplay or runtime selection changes.

Rebuild from the trader root: `python build-animations.py --age EarlyMedieval`. Validate with `python validate-early-medieval.py`. Editable registration: rig-authoring.json. Moving-ground GIF and phase/size review are in Review.
