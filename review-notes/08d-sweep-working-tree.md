## How it works today (what this change set does)

The working tree bundles at least eight independent pieces of work:

1. **Live-match freeze fix (Trade publication).** `Trade.currentMarkets()` (Trade.ts:346-367) is a pure read view built from `buildingFacts().completed(owner,type)`; `refreshMarkets()` (Trade.ts:368-380) uses it for authoritative state and `siteSnapshot()` (Trade.ts:1001-1017) rebuilds the view per player on every publication so a building whose owner changed to 0 at end of tick is no longer dereferenced through `progression.states[0]`. `maxCargo` is now `Σ supply.goodsCapacity(b)`.
2. **Blast-edge ownership on defeat.** `checkWinner()` (Simulation.ts:5440-5474) computes `recipient` once and, after transferring owned tiles, reassigns every remaining building in `buildingIndex.byOwner(player.id)` to `recipient` (0 under raid rules without capture permission, else the beneficiary). HUD labels owner-0 buildings "Neutral" (HudViewModel.ts:778). `OnlineMatchSession` routes a non-completed `match-ended` through `fail()` (OnlineMatchSession.ts:129) so commands are disabled.
3. **Pre-modern balance revision (rules version 12).** `FORMATION_RULES_VERSION` 11→12 (Manpower.ts:5); soldier-weapons schema 2 with nullable `melee`/`ranged`, per-channel `bonuses`, optional `rangeTiles` and `accuracy` (compileSoldierWeapons.ts:4-25, SoldierWeapons.ts:4-20, 26-66). New `content/unit-tuning.json` + `applyUnitTuning()` (UnitTuning.ts:21-28) applied in `Units.ts:411` before soldier weapons. `CONTENT_HASH` bumps `combatRevision` 8→10 and `aiCombatPolicyRevision` 2→4 (Catalog.ts:110,117). Eight ranged units now have no melee weapon (verified from plans). I re-ran the zod schema over `skirmish/plans/*.json` in a scratch script: compiled `soldier-weapons.json` is byte-identical to the compile output; `formation-recipes.json` is semantically identical.
4. **Weapon accuracy.** `weaponAccuracy(profile, distance)` (WeaponAccuracy.ts:3-8) linearly interpolates `accuracy.near→far` over `range`; applied to projectile `attackScale` at launch (Battle.ts:372-374) and to direct per-soldier ranged hits (Battle.ts:999). Only the two Napoleonic firearms author it (90%→35%).
5. **Contact-limited melee (combat revision 10).** `meleeReceivers()` (MeleeDamage.ts:21-104) enumerates exposed target slots by projecting formation slots on the contact normal and screening them by foreground silhouettes; `MeleeDamageBatch` (MeleeDamage.ts:118-175) pools all melee claims in a tick, divides each exposed body's HP budget proportionally and discards overkill. `Battle.meleeHit()` (Battle.ts:616-626) routes every melee hit against a soldier-model, non-afloat target into the batch; `fight()` resolves it before `awardDamage` (Battle.ts:1083-1086). Ranged-only soldiers in contact now reserve a geometry-only `RANGED_CONTACT` profile (Battle.ts:67-71, 583) so they neither strike nor fire; `hybridContacts` therefore runs for every per-soldier ranged squad (Battle.ts:918), not only melee+ranged hybrids.
6. **Trade cargo / port storage.** `tradeCargoEffects`/`tradeCargoCapacity` (TradeCargo.ts) factor the former inline math; `Supply.goodsCapacity()` (Supply.ts:137-141) caps a port's stock at one unstacked shipload at its owner's research (e.g. 20 goods in the Stone Age vs the previous 1000). Factories keep 1000.
7. **Tribal expansion operations.** `AiOperations.enabled()` now includes tribes whenever `deferredPlanning` is on (AiOperations.ts:99-102), which is the default policy (`AiPolicies.ts:5`). New per-record `frontier`, `tribe`, `campaign`, `retries`, `reason` fields (AiOperations.ts:44-51); `expansionBlocked()` reads `world.hasUnclaimedFrontier()` (O(1), FactionAdjacency.ts:10) and a 30 s growth high-water mark; `recruitmentObjective()` feeds `AiEconomicSnapshot.invasion` → `militaryDemand` → committed recruit candidates. `Simulation.thinkAi` lets a tribe with a committed invasion pursue its target beyond the patrol radius (Simulation.ts:4946-4949, 5004-5005, 5033, 5125-5131). `Expansion.thinkTribeDevelopment` holds gold for one producible recruit and prefers warfare research/military buildings while militarizing (Expansion.ts:1512-1522, 1541-1549).
8. **Human territorial entry.** The raid-rules war-on-entry loop (Simulation.ts:3596-3604) now includes afloat squads and declares on any committed movement while standing in non-allied land, not only on a tile change.
9. **Presentation.** `CombatPresentationTimeline` (client) interpolates between the last ≤4 snapshots and applies head-count/strike facts only when the playhead reaches the endpoint; `ArcherVolley.combatRoots` + `PackedSnapshotDetails.volleyRoots` (Protocol.ts:401-402, SnapshotDetails.ts:44, 372-373, 428-435, 747-765) carry exact carrier positions at impact so a squad removed by the hit can finish its movement. `TroopActors` picks casualties by distance to the attacked edge (`TroopCasualties.ts`) and freezes corpses at the last drawn pose. Automatic production reserves now grow with producer counts for humans and AI alike (AutomaticProduction.ts:201-205), continuous manual orders run above reserve, and AI demand takes the max with the baseline instead of replacing it (AutomaticProduction.ts:266-271).
10. **Lab tooling.** `BattleLabModel` (disposable arena with per-squad local definitions via `BattleWorld.unitDefinition`, Battle.ts:73-74, 221-223), `BalancePreview.ts` page, `UnitLabSheet` spreadsheet editor, new Vite entry `balancePreview`.

### High

**3. AI strategic payload production is starved by the new 100-kit floor.** `src/skirmish/domain/AutomaticProduction.ts:203` changed `troopTarget` from `max(context.ai ? 12 : 100, …)` to `max(100, …)` for AI too, and :266-271 no longer clears targets when `aiDemand` is present (`Math.max(targets.get(recipe) ?? 0, requested)`). CONFIRMED by `tests/skirmish/AiLateGame.test.ts:142` ("Salvos use spare industrial capacity after the army's kit buffers are full" → no `make-hydrogen`/`make-mirv` job). Consequence at the 14-nation target: every AI nation diverts arms-factory cycles and materials into a ≥100-kit (up to 600) reserve before it will ever build a warhead, and spends gold on reserves instead of buildings; late-game AI threat collapses. Fix: keep the AI floor at its demand quote (restore `context.ai ? 12 : 100` or `aiDemand ? 0 : …`) or exempt payload recipes from the reserve ordering.

**4. The formation-doctrine compiler now rejects the shipped recipes.** `scripts/FormationDoctrineSchema.ts:57` throws when a mounted line's frontage exceeds `FRONTAGE.mountedWidthRatioMax` of contemporary infantry; with `bronzeage-cavalry` capacity raised in `skirmish/plans/formation-recipes.json` the ratio is 1.889. CONFIRMED by `tests/skirmish/FlexibleFrontage.test.ts:74`. Consequence: `npx tsx scripts/compileFormationDoctrines.ts` cannot be re-run; the next doctrine edit is blocked and the committed `formation-doctrines` output is out of sync with its validator. Fix: raise the ratio for Bronze Age or reduce Bronze cavalry capacity, and re-run all three compile scripts together.

**5. Melee hits with no exposed receiver are silently discarded.** `src/skirmish/domain/MeleeDamage.ts:130-131,141,162`: `add()` slices `slots.slice(0, ceil(contributors))` and `resolve()` `continue`s on an empty slot list, so a claim whose `meleeReceivers()` returned `[]` applies zero damage. `Battle.meleeHit` (Battle.ts:616-626) calls it only after `legalContact`/`participants` approved the strike, but those use contact pools and footprints while `meleeReceivers` uses `formationSlots` projected on the contact normal plus `polygonSweepEntry` with `reach + penetration + CONTACT.release`. SUSPECT: any disagreement between the two geometries (oblique wedge/column contact, a target mid-`formation.transition`, a hull-vs-soldier mismatch after promotion) produces a strike that animates (`meleeStrike` is still recorded) but deals nothing, with no counter or log. Consequence: intermittent "immortal" formations that are impossible to diagnose from a live match. Mitigation: when `slots.length === 0` but `hit > 0`, fall back to the pool path capped at one body per contributor, and add a test that scans every authored shape pair at contact and asserts `meleeReceivers` is non-empty whenever `participants() > 0`.

**6. Strategic sleep key now depends on an index that diverges after restore.** `src/skirmish/domain/AiOperations.ts:324-325` folds `expansionBlocked()` into `sleepKey`; `expansionBlocked` (:123-129) reads `world.hasUnclaimedFrontier()` = `FactionAdjacency.unclaimedEdges` (FactionAdjacency.ts:10), which is maintained incrementally in `changeOwner` (Simulation.ts:4836) and recomputed from scratch in `restore` (Simulation.ts:309). CONFIRMED by `tests/skirmish/ResponsiveGameplay.test.ts:119`: after `clone.restore(checkpoint)` the clone's key reads `…:true:5` while the live sim reads `…:false:0`, so `nextThink` differs (1426 vs 1480) and the two simulations plan differently from that tick on. The fixture writes `game.owners.fill()` directly (ResponsiveGameplay.test.ts:36), which leaves the live counters stale — but that is exactly the drift the `changed()`→`rebuild()` fallback (FactionAdjacency.ts:33-36) exists to absorb, and the same divergence occurs whenever any code path writes `owners[]` without `changeOwner` (several tests and the conquest/fallout paths do). Consequence: a restored match (or a verifier replay) makes different AI war decisions than the live run; checkpoint verification tests become flaky. Fix: either persist `frontier`/blocked state only through the record (it already is) and drop `blocked` from the key, or make `restore`-equivalence a tested invariant by rebuilding adjacency in the fixture.
