## How it works today

### Who decides what (call tree from the tick)

`Skirmish.step()` (Simulation.ts:3414-3425) per tick:

1. `promoteTribes()` (5415) — every tick, O(players): a tribe with `land ≥ 10 %` of land tiles (`canPromoteTribe`, FactionRules.ts:67) is re-labelled `kind="regular"` in place.
2. `expansion.beforeStep()` (Expansion.ts:1085):
   - `diplomacy.step` (Diplomacy.ts:287) expires embargoes (60 s after last theft), peace protections, offers, alliances, wars of eliminated factions.
   - `economy.step()` (AiEconomicDirector.ts:196) — the nation AI pipeline, **nations only** (`enabled` = `aiEconomy && ai && kind==="regular" && !eliminated`, line 99):
     - `expireOwnership` (499): drops ledger reservations and asset leases whose `expiresTick` passed, whose owner generation changed, or whose entity is gone.
     - `recovery.step` (AiRecovery.ts:112) every 3 s: un-sticks squads stuck in `movementStatus "restricted"` for 6 s, rallies idle squads to a core building after losses, fallout cleanup.
     - Fact readers under one shared allowance: `boundaries.step(32)` (only while rebuilding), `fronts.step(16)` (AiFrontRecords: one 16×16 sector cell per unit), `navalFacts.step(48-front)`, `naval.step(16)` (AiNavalPlanner: one nation per tick), `military.step(≤24)` → `AiArmyPlanner.step` (one nation per tick: `invasion.step(≤8)` + mission housekeeping + ≤3 missions × `missionQuantum 8`), `tradeQuotes.step(≤8)`, `coasts.step(≤8)`, `cities.step(≤128-rest)`.
     - `defenses.step()` (AiDefenseDirector.ts:528): every 2 ticks (`secondsToTicks(.15)`=2 at 8 Hz), one nation (round-robin), advances/proposes a tower-and-wall project around one city; `modernFronts.step(32)` one nation per tick.
     - Every 2 ticks, one nation whose `nextDecision` (3 s) elapsed: `decide(player)` — `economicSnapshot` (O(own) + threat scan over *all* world squads, line 285-297), `militaryDemand`, development goals and paid build/recruit commands.
   - `thinkProgression()` (Expansion.ts:1315) — every tick loops all players; a player acts when `tick % 24 === (id*3) % 24`: `thinkPeace` (1067), then for tribes `thinkTribeDevelopment` (1512), for nations research/advance-age, building priority list with `economicBuildingTarget`, `thinkCapabilities` (modernization leases, ship recruitment), `thinkAirAndStrategic`, answering alliance offers via `acceptsAlliance`, and (allied victory mode only) proactive alliance offers.
3. `advanceDiplomaticWithdrawal()` → `stepDiplomaticWithdrawal` (AiDiplomaticWithdrawal.ts:135): an AI squad standing on land it may no longer enter gets a 30 s withdrawal obligation persisted on the squad (`aiWithdrawal`).
4. `thinkAi()` (Simulation.ts:4867) — **all AI factions, every tick**:
   - Groups all squads by owner (O(S)), computes `forces` (ready squads per faction, O(S)) and calls `operations.step(forces)` (AiOperations.ts:302).
   - Per AI player: `develop` every 3 s (`tick%24===id%24`): legacy `developAi`/`developTribeAi` only when `!expansion`; recruitment of one squad for factions without the economy director (i.e. **tribes**) through `expansion.recruitment` and the normal `recruit` command (4887-4921).
   - If any squad is due this tick (`(tick+squad.id)%6===1` with the default 0.75 s interval): registers bridgehead anchors (O(own)), reads `homeTerritory.frontier` (BFS over own territory, cached 3 s), `homeNeedsLand`, then per due squad not owned by an Army/lease: cancels illegal attack/move orders, tribe leash, spatial query (20 tiles) for the best hostile target by distance + approach load, embark/escape logic across water, replenish, intercept (`attack` order), else raid cohort (nations or tribes with a committed campaign) or march cohort (expansion toward frontier). Cohorts of ≤16 squads become `routeWork.request("raid:…"/"march:…", kind "ai-move")` jobs that are validated again at execution against the current `controlGenerations` and footprint policy (4047-4051).
   - `thinkNavy` only when the naval planner is disabled.

### Strategic layer: `AiOperations`

One record per enabled AI (`enabled`: `aiWarPolicy && ai && !eliminated && (regular || deferredPlanning)`, so tribes are included). Phases `peace → preparing → war → recovery → peace`. Per tick it validates target/threat lists for every record; a record *thinks* only when `tick ≥ nextThink` (3 s + `id % 8` ticks; 1 s in war; 10 s when the sleep key is unchanged). A think: `refreshTheatres` (defense/screen/offense theatres fed to the army planner), `expansionBlocked` (territorial high-water mark), `forceReadiness`, `invasionTroops` near each threat, phase logic, and the candidate scan (≤4 rivals per think, ≤16 reads per tick across all factions, cursor persisted). Scores: `dist²(base,base)/(advantage·opportunity·(retaliating?2:1)) + commitments·4096`. `combatAdvantage` is `engagementEstimate` over per-definition cohorts. Transition to war goes through `diplomacy.establishWar(…,"invasion")` (raids rules), which is the **only** way the AI ever declares war; `beginAiRaid` (Diplomacy.ts:57) has no caller.

Threats: `notifyHostileAction` (Simulation.ts:588) from damage/capture commits, coast goals and naval sightings push `{rival,tile,until:+30 s}` into the victim's record and wake it. Permissions (`canEnter`/`canPursue`/`canTarget`) under raids rules reduce to `diplomacy.hostile` (= non-allied ∧ not protected ∧ (declared war ∨ embargo)).

### Operational layer (nations only)

- `AiArmyPlanner` (one nation per tick): turns theatres into `land-army` missions (cap `min(armies.capacity, 1+⌊squads/40⌋, 4)`), selects rosters from a snapshot of squad ids, leases them in `AiAssetLeases` (priority `operation`/`defense`), creates a real Army through `create-army`, and drives `rally → assemble → advance → engage/flank/breach → recover → replenish/refit → complete` with the same `army-order` commands a human would send.
- `AiInvasionResponse` (inside the planner's per-nation slot): on the nearest threat, leases up to `max(4, 2·⌈pressure/1000⌉)` (or everything when *blocking*: last city endangered or pressure > max(2000, ready/3)) with priority `emergency-defense` and moves them to the closest city, then to the threat.
- `AiDefenseDirector` / `AiModernFronts`: paid tower circuits and Modern trench sections, staffed through leases.
- `AiMilitaryDirector.decide` (called from the economy decision): modernization refit leases.
- `AiNavalPlanner`: one fleet mission per nation.

### Persistent state and checkpoints

Checkpointed (Expansion.ts:146, AiEconomicDirector.ts:117-140): operations records + theatres + revisions, army missions (`missions`, `byPlayer`, cursors, serial), invasion responses, defense projects/allowances/outlines, modern sections, front records/regions, boundary chunks, naval facts/missions, ledger reservations, asset leases, loss windows, recovery episodes, city records, placements, demands, `tribePlans`, diplomatic geography, pair-relations recent aggression, diplomacy state (+ AI cooldowns), `controlGenerations`, `HomeTerritory` cache/cursors/bridgeheads, `aiWithdrawal` on squads. Transient (recomputed): `AiOperations.navigationKeys`, `PairRelations` projections, footprint caches (drain-local), `HomeTerritory.visited`. Validation on restore is strict for missions (AiArmyPlanner.ts:86-95) and diplomacy (DiplomacyValidation.ts).

### Command flow

Every AI decision ends in `world.applyCommand({playerId,…})` (or `applyArmyCommand`), i.e. the same validator humans use (Simulation.ts:1249, Expansion.ts:431). AI-specific restrictions live inside the validator (`player.ai` → cannot delete buildings 1264, cannot manually sail warships 1258, admission gets an `ai` flag 1472). Movement legality for AI additionally passes `aiFootprintAllowed` at admission, route-task execution and movement rollback (3582-3585). Budgets (`BUDGETS`, TacticalRules.ts:52) bound mission work; `RouteWork` drains 24 route jobs per tick for everyone.

### Tribes vs nations (rules)

Rule differences (FactionRules.ts, TribeDevelopment.ts, Diplomacy.ts:172): squad cap `10 + 5·ageIndex` vs 60-200; trader cap `16 + 2·age`; warship cap 24 vs 32; one copy of each building (two of military, unlimited mines); age advance only once 75 % of surviving non-tribes reached that age; tribes cannot make or receive alliance offers (only `declare` and peace actions); promotion by 10 % land or by being the conquest beneficiary of an AI nation (inherits completed research, Simulation.ts:5444-5448). AI differences: no economy director, army planner, defense, naval planner or invasion response; `thinkTribeDevelopment` (one building type per pass, ≤16 site tries, military order under `promotionPressure`), recruitment in `thinkAi`, per-squad leash to 8 tiles outside own land (`TRIBE_INTERCEPT_RANGE`), promotion campaign via the tribe branch of `AiOperations` (peaceful growth first, 30 s without growth → may prepare against a weaker tribe/vulnerable nation; `desiredSquads` from `advantage`, certified route, `fieldAdvantage ≥ 1.1`, stalls after 90 s w/o progress).

### Diplomacy the AI uses

Raids-v1 rules (`Diplomacy.raids`): hostility is war ∨ embargo (theft in the last 60 s). AI: declares war only by invasion; answers alliance offers with `acceptsAlliance` (regular proposer, land ratio, not under betrayal penalty, ally cap); proposes alliances only in allied-victory matches; answers and proposes peace through `thinkPeace` (willing unless *in `war` phase against you and stronger in total troops*); never starts raids, never uses `dont-embargo`/`allow-embargo`, never breaks alliances. Humans: auto-steal from any adjacent non-allied trader (Trade.ts:1234, `automaticHuman`) which creates an embargo = hostility; moving a ship or squad into non-allied land *declares war* automatically (Simulation.ts:3431, 3604); peace offers expire in 20 s and give 60 s protection.

## Strengths

- Clean separation of **decision policy** from **authority**: every AI action is an ordinary command; permissions are read from `Diplomacy`, never duplicated (AiOperations.ts:53 comment is true in the code).
- Generation fencing is consistently applied: leases, reservations, missions, projects, responses and route tasks all carry `generation` and are dropped on controller handover (`setAiController`, AiAssetLeases.expire, AiBudgetLedger.expire, AiArmyPlanner.ts:172/201, AiDefenseDirector.ts:564, AiModernFronts.ts:494, Simulation.ts:4047).
- Ids rather than references everywhere in persistent AI state; stale ids are re-resolved on use (`world.squad(id)`, `funded()`, `valid()`), so the Trade-style stale-reference bug class is largely absent in the AI code.
- Bounded, resumable work with explicit cursors (`AiFrontRecords`, `AiDefenseOutline`, `DiplomaticGeography`, candidate scan) and global per-tick caps (16 candidate reads, 112/128 read-model units, 24 mission units).
- Thoughtful anti-oscillation devices: territorial high-water (`expansionBlocked`), campaign progress with historical minima (`updateCampaign`), retry cooldowns per target, sleep keys, `AiLossWindow` buckets.
- Deterministic tie-breaks (`|| a.id - b.id`) are habitual.

## Issues

### I1. Invasion response steals army-mission squads and the planner responds by releasing *everything* — commitment thrash  
`src/skirmish/domain/AiInvasionResponse.ts:139-145, 172-184`; `src/skirmish/domain/AiArmyPlanner.ts:309-315`; `AiAssetLeases.ts:50-59`. Severity **high**, confidence **high (CONFIRMED by trace)**.  
Mechanism: the response roster accepts squads whose lease priority is `patrol`/`operation`/`defense` (i.e. squads owned by land-army or defense missions) and acquires them with `emergency-defense` (3 > 1/2), so the acquisition succeeds and overwrites the army's lease. On the army mission's next step, `members.some(s => !assets.owns(…, plan.id))` is true and the planner calls `this.release(player.id)` — which releases **all** missions of that nation, disbands their Armies, *and* `invasion.release(playerId)` (line 113) deletes the response itself. The response is recreated at the nation's next slot (≈ 1.75 s with 14 nations), re-rosters, and can grab army squads again. Any non-blocking border skirmish (pressure ≤ max(2000, ready/3)) therefore repeatedly destroys offensives for the 30 s threat window.  
Consequence: AI offensives collapse whenever its border is poked; armies are created/disbanded in a loop (route churn, `army-order` churn); defensive response is also reset every cycle so it never finishes gathering. Repro sketch: nation with a `land-army` mission in `advance` whose roster includes squads near its capital city; have a hostile squad damage a border tile within 16 tiles of that city for 30 s; watch `missions` count and `armies.armies` oscillate.

### I2. `AiArmyPlanner.stepMission` uses a player-wide release for a single inconsistent mission  
`src/skirmish/domain/AiArmyPlanner.ts:313`. Severity **medium**, confidence **high**.  
Independent of I1: a missing army (e.g. disbanded by `armies` logic), an empty roster or any single foreign lease wipes all missions and the invasion response for the faction. Should be `releaseMission(plan)`.

### I3. Nations never attack nations while any neutral frontier edge exists  
`src/skirmish/domain/AiOperations.ts:405, 423`; `FactionAdjacency.ts:10`. Severity **high (gameplay)**, confidence **high**.  
`expanding = raids && !threats.length && hasUnclaimedFrontier(player)` ignores the `blocked` high-water test computed two lines earlier, and `hasUnclaimedFrontier` is true if a single owned walkable tile touches a single neutral walkable tile. With `expanding`, a non-retaliating nation needs `advantage ≥ 2.5` (conqueror 1.6) against anyone that is not a tribe. On 500×250 / 1000×500 maps neutral pockets persist for most of a match (claims stall on contested or far tiles), so nation-vs-nation and nation-vs-human wars happen almost only as retaliation (threats, embargoes, entry wars) or against tribes. Consequence: "AI never attacks" except when provoked; combined with I9 the human becomes the provoked target of choice.

### I4. `AiModernFronts` assessment restarts whenever the global squad count changes; at target scale it never completes  
`src/skirmish/domain/AiModernFronts.ts:243-257, 549-551`. Severity **medium**, confidence **high**.  
The assessment scans `world.squads` 32 per visit, one nation per tick (`break` at 551), and is reset when `assessment.length !== world.squads.length`. With 1100 squads and 14 nations a section needs ~35 visits ≈ 490 ticks ≈ 61 s with no squad spawning or dying anywhere on the map. Modern trench sections are therefore effectively dead at the target load (sections sit in `staffing` forever).

### I5. `Diplomacy.hostile` is a linear scan of alliances, protections, wars and embargoes and is called in the hottest loops  
`src/skirmish/domain/Diplomacy.ts:31-36, 117-130`; callers e.g. `Simulation.ts:458` (`collisionHostile`), 5032, 5155, `Trade.ts:1234`, `AiFrontRecords.ts:122/197`. Severity **medium (scalability)**, confidence **high for the mechanism, medium for the magnitude**.  
With 52 factions the wars/embargoes/protection arrays can hold dozens to hundreds of rows; each `hostile` evaluates `allied` (scan alliances) + `protected` (scan) + `declaredWar` (scan wars) + `embargo` (scan). It is invoked per squad pair in collision/separation, per nearby enemy in `thinkAi` (≈180 due squads × nearby), in trade capture, in front scans. This is the O(factions² × calls) term in the AI/combat path. `PairRelations` already builds a per-pair projection with a revision stamp (PairRelations.ts:641) but `hostile` does not use it.

### I6. `AiOperations` sleep key embeds every faction's force count — the 10 s sleep is dead at scale  
`src/skirmish/domain/AiOperations.ts:309, 325-326`. Severity **low-medium (perf)**, confidence **high**.  
`ageKey` concatenates `forces.get(p.id)` for all players; with 1100 squads at least one ready-count changes nearly every tick, so `sleepKey` never matches and every peaceful AI thinks every 3 s (+ `forceReadiness`, `refreshTheatres`, `combatCohorts` of up to 4 rivals with `formationEnvelope` per squad). Also `ageKey` and `diplomacyKey = JSON.stringify([alliances, wars])` are rebuilt every tick for all 44 AIs even when no record is due (lines 309-310).

### I7. Stagger slots collapse at 8 Hz — strategic thinks bunch into 8 ticks  
`src/skirmish/domain/Expansion.ts:1319` (`(player.id*3) % 24`), `AiOperations.ts:174/295/403/441` (`id % TICKS_PER_SECOND`), `Simulation.ts:4880` (`id % 24`). Severity **low-medium (latency spikes)**, confidence **high**.  
`3·id mod 24` has only 8 distinct values, so 52 factions share 8 slots: ~6-7 `thinkProgression` passes (site searches, `thinkAirAndStrategic` scans of all squads/buildings, peace offers) land in the same tick every 3 s. `id % TICKS_PER_SECOND` similarly only spreads over 8 ticks. At 10 Hz this was 10 slots; the 8 Hz change made it worse.

### I8. Human peace offers are a near-unconditional shield  
`src/skirmish/domain/Expansion.ts:1067-1077`; `Diplomacy.ts:76-83, 156-166`; `AiOperations.ts:318-320`. Severity **medium (exploit)**, confidence **high**.  
`willing(other)` is true unless the AI is already in `war` phase against you *and* its total alive troops ≥ yours. Any AI in `preparing` (which lasts ≥ assembly+4 s plus route certification, visible as "Preparing invasion" in its reason) accepts, giving 60 s protection; AiOperations then forces `recovery` (30 s) and later a fresh scan. Even in `war`, a human with more total troops anywhere on the map (not at the front) gets peace while the AI is winning locally. Only the 300 s `peacePairNext` cooldown per pair limits it; across 14 nations that is one free "stop" per AI every 5 minutes.

### I9. Human-initiated hostility is automatic and frequent, and it halves the AI's target score for that human  
`src/skirmish/Simulation.ts:3431, 3604`; `Trade.ts:1234`; `AiOperations.ts:419-421, 429`. Severity **medium (gameplay)**, confidence **medium (mechanism confirmed, outcome SUSPECT)**.  
A human squad/ship entering any non-allied tile declares war (`cause:"entry"`); a human unit adjacent to any non-allied trader auto-steals and creates an embargo. Each of these makes the human `retaliating` for that AI, which doubles the denominator of the distance score and bypasses the `expanding` veto (I3). Net effect: humans who move around are the only targets AI nations are allowed to pick while frontier exists — the classic "everyone attacks the human" outcome, produced by design rather than by intent.

### I10. Alliance acceptance is cheap for a large human and uncapped on the human side  
`src/skirmish/domain/AiPersonality.ts:76-91`; `Expansion.ts:1440-1454`. Severity **low-medium (exploit)**, confidence **high**.  
AI accepts any offer from a regular faction with land ≥ ratio × AI land (0.4-1.25) unless under a 30-120 s betrayal penalty, up to its `maximumAllies`. The human has no ally cap, no cost, and allied AIs can never target them (`nonAllied` in candidate scan). A strong human can neutralise every neighbour for 300 s per treaty and renew.

### I11. AI raids are dead code; AI never embargoes, never blocks trade, never breaks a treaty  
`src/skirmish/domain/Diplomacy.ts:57-66` (`beginAiRaid`, no caller in `src/skirmish`). Severity **low-medium (design gap)**, confidence **high**.  
Under raids rules the only AI-initiated hostility is a full invasion war. There is no low-intensity AI behaviour (raid, embargo, harass) although the rule set was built for it — exactly the layer nomads/pirates need.

### I12. Eliminated nations leak planner state (missions, projects, demands) until match end  
`src/skirmish/domain/AiArmyPlanner.ts:167-172, 189`; `AiDefenseDirector.ts:535`; `AiEconomicDirector.ts:227-236`. Severity **low**, confidence **high**.  
`economy.release(playerId)` is only invoked from `setAiController` and `decide()`; `decide` is never reached for a non-enabled (eliminated) player, and the planner's housekeeping (line 172) runs only for the selected enabled player. Missions of eliminated nations stay in `missions`/`byPlayer` (and in every checkpoint), `defenses.projects`, `demands`, `saving`, `nextDecision` likewise. Leases/reservations do expire. With pop-up factions that spawn and die repeatedly this becomes unbounded growth.

### I13. Controller handover human→AI leaves human-created Armies and queued orders orphaned  
`src/skirmish/Simulation.ts:226-239`; `AiArmyPlanner.ts:134` (`!armies.armyOf`), `Simulation.ts:4993` (`controllerOf(...) !== "none" → continue`). Severity **medium**, confidence **medium (SUSPECT: not run)**.  
On disconnect (`serverMatchWorker.ts:247`) the generation is bumped and economy/operations are released, but Armies the human created are neither disbanded nor adopted: the planner only manages missions it created and `eligible` excludes squads in any army; `thinkAi` skips squads with a controller. Those squads keep their last army order and are never used again by the AI (no attack, no defence) unless the army is auto-controlled. Same for the reverse direction only partially (AI armies are disbanded by `armyPlanner.release`).

### I14. Tribe promotion threshold and sequencing make 30 tribes mostly inert speed-bumps  
`src/skirmish/FactionRules.ts:65-77`; `AiOperations.ts:417-418, 428`; `Expansion.ts:1862-1874`. Severity **medium (design)**, confidence **medium**.  
10 % of all land tiles for promotion (e.g. ~6 000 tiles on Mediterranean) is not reachable for a 10-15-squad faction with one city; promotion in practice happens only by inheriting a defeated AI nation. Nations get `tribeExpansion` (opportunity 3, no readiness checks) against tribes whenever `advantage ≥ 1.5`, so tribes are the AI's main war targets (also the only ones not blocked by I3). Expected arc: tribes are eaten in the first ages, then no tribes remain for the late game; in allied-victory mode `coalition()` additionally requires every tribe dead.

### I15. A tribe boxed in by a stronger neighbour cycles prepare(90 s)/recover(30 s)/rescan forever at full military priority  
`src/skirmish/domain/AiOperations.ts:386-387, 129-136, 409`. Severity **low**, confidence **high**.  
`retryLater` blocks the target for 60 s from the *failure* tick, but recovery already takes 30 s and the rescan a few more seconds, so the same hopeless target is re-selected on the next pass; `promotionPressure` keeps the tribe on `TRIBE_MILITARY_*` orders meanwhile. Bounded but wasteful and it starves the tribe's own economy.

### I16. `thinkAi` per-squad raid branch sorts the whole enemy building list per decision  
`src/skirmish/Simulation.ts:5137-5140, 5154-5159`. Severity **low (perf)**, confidence **high**.  
For every due squad in raid mode: `conquestBuildings(buildingIndex.byOwner(enemy))` filtered through `aiFootprintAllowed` and sorted by distance (O(B log B)), plus a possible `spatial.nearest` ring scan over the whole grid when no building qualifies (`nearest` visits rings until it finds an accepted item — unbounded when none exists, SpatialGrid.ts:217). With ~180 due squads per tick this is the dominant `thinkAi` term for nations without the army planner (tribes on campaign, nations below `raidAfterTicks`).

### I17. `AiOperations.step` candidate loop indexes `world.players` by cursor — safe only while the players array is append-only  
`src/skirmish/domain/AiOperations.ts:407-408`. Severity **low (forward-looking)**, confidence **high**.  
Pop-up factions that are removed from `players` (or re-ordered) would skip/duplicate candidates for every record with a saved cursor. Keep the array append-only or store the candidate cursor as a player id.

### I18. Test environment: every `restore()`-based test fails on this machine  
`src/skirmish/Simulation.ts:245-246`. Severity **medium (process)**, confidence **high that it fails, medium on cause**.  
`tiles instanceof Uint8Array` is false for a `structuredClone`d typed array under the jsdom vitest environment (vite.config.ts:313) on Node 24.18; 16 AI tests plus 4 `Conquest` tests fail with "Invalid checkpoint tile array". The AI checkpoint-continuity tests (the ones guarding this bug class) are therefore not currently proving anything locally. A realm-safe check (`ArrayBuffer.isView(tiles) && tiles.BYTES_PER_ELEMENT === 1` or `Object.prototype.toString.call(tiles) === "[object Uint8Array]"`) would fix it.

### Not found (looked for explicitly)
- NaN from empty rosters: `combatAdvantage` short-circuits (0/8), `refreshTheatres` divides by `max(1,count)`, `formationTile` returns centre for `count ≤ 1`, invasion/army reserves use `max(1, …)`. No division-by-zero path found in scope.
- Budgets never refilled: defense wall allowance accrues by elapsed time (capped 6 s), ledger/leases expire by tick, `retries` are sliced, `HomeTerritory.failures` capped at 2048. OK.
- Stale squad ids: all persisted ids are re-resolved before use.

## Design: AI architecture for 8 humans + 14 nations + 30 tribes + nomads + pirates

### Principles that should survive
- Keep "decision = command through the same validator"; keep generation fencing and id-based persistence; keep a single global per-tick read budget. Pop-up factions must be *players* (ids ≤ `MAX_PLAYER_ID`, which is 64 today: 20+14+30 — raise to 80 and reserve a band for pop-ups so the `Uint8Array` owners map and `FactionAdjacency` sizes stay valid).
- Add `FactionKind = "regular" | "tribe" | "nomad" | "pirate"` (FactionRoster/Factions.ts, Protocol `Player.kind`) and route every `kind === "tribe"` special case through a small `FactionProfile` table (`canOwnLand`, `canBuild`, `squadCap`, `victoryEligible`, `canAlly`, `promotesTo`, `despawnWhen`), so Trade, Diplomacy, Conquest, HUD and victory stop branching on string equality.

### Shared with the existing AI
- **Strategy record**: `AiOperations` record per faction with a new phase set for roaming kinds (`roam → raid → extort → settle/hired → despawn`) but the same `threats`, `retries`, `nextThink`, checkpoint and the 16-read candidate budget. Candidate scoring reuses `combatAdvantage`, `geography.eligible`, `commitments`.
- **Routes**: `economy.routes.request(id, playerId, from, to, …)` (`AiRouteQuotes`) for migration legs and raid approaches; `RouteWork` ai-move tasks for cohorts.
- **Leases/ledger**: pop-ups have no economy but should still lease their squads/ships through `AiAssetLeases` so `thinkAi` and a future `AiRaidPlanner` cannot double-order them.
- **Personality/doctrine**: add two personality ids (`raider`, `corsair`) in AiPersonalities with `interceptRange`, `minimumRaidSquads`, `replenishBelow`; doctrines with `engagement: "fire-retreat"`.
- **Diplomacy**: raids-v1 already models embargo/war/protection/peace; pop-ups use `beginAiRaid` for low-intensity hostility and `establishWar` only when attacked. Tribute = a new `alliance` action pair `offer-tribute`/`accept-tribute` that creates `peaceProtection` for N seconds in exchange for gold (validated in `Diplomacy.action`, state in `DiplomacyState`).

### What must be new
- **Faction lifecycle** (new `domain/PopupFactions.ts`, stepped from `Expansion.beforeStep`): spawn rules (nomads: from the Bronze Age, at most `⌈nations/4⌉` alive, spawn on a neutral land tile ≥ 24 tiles from any city reachable from ≥ 2 factions; pirates: from Classical, at most `⌈ports/6⌉`, spawn on neutral water near an active sea trade lane from `AiNavalFacts`), despawn rules (no squads/ships for 30 s, or settle/hire), a per-match `FactionRoster` deck for names, deterministic from the match PRNG. Elimination of pop-ups must not award territory or trigger `conquest` announcements; `checkWinner` excludes `victoryEligible=false` kinds from `survivors` and `coalition()` ignores them.
- **Moving camp for nomads**: a `camp` tile on the player (`player.base` already exists; add `campMovedTick`) with a small claim radius (reuse `TRIBE_BASE_RADIUS` claims via `NeutralTerritoryClaims`) that is re-anchored on each migration leg; no buildings (or one "tent" building type with zero footprint that replicates as the camp marker). Settling = `kind → "tribe"` in place (same trick as `promoteTribes`), granting a city at the camp.
- **Hire**: `alliance` action `hire` from a nation/human: pays gold, pop-up becomes `allied` for 180 s and its `AiOperations.target` is set to the hirer's current war target; expiry returns to `roam`.
- **Pirates**: ships only (`shipCap` by kind), objective = patrol lanes from `AiNavalFacts` and intercept traders; theft already works through `Trade.ts:1234` (`canSteal` with embargo/war); add `canSteal` short-circuit `kind==="pirate" → true` against non-allied, non-protected owners. Pirates despawn when their last ship dies; they can be "bought off" with tribute (protection) by coastal factions.
- **Replication/HUD**: `Player.kind` is already replicated; add camp/lane markers to the snapshot (`ExpansionSnapshot`), a HUD badge for pop-ups, and announcements `{kind:"popup", action:"spawn"|"despawn"|"settle"|"hired"}` through `announce`.
- **Budget**: nomads ≤ 8 squads, pirates ≤ 6 ships; strategic think every 3 s under the existing 16-read cap; one shared `AiRaidPlanner.step(8)` slot per tick (round-robin over pop-ups) — total ≈ +10 % of today's AI tick cost at 6 pop-ups. No per-tile scans: lanes from `AiNavalFacts`, targets from `tradeFacts`/`squadFacts().byOwner`.
- **Determinism/checkpoints**: all new state in a `popups` section of `Expansion.checkpoint` (version bump of `Skirmish.checkpoint` to 12 with a tolerant reader for 11); ids from `allocateId`; PRNG from `this.random` only; player array append-only (I17).
