# V1.1 AI, movement and progression review

This is the requested separate design pass after the overnight stability investigation. The eleven gameplay changes below are proposed deliverables, not features implemented by the stability patch. Measurements and release status belong in `2026-10-03-hosted-late-game-investigation.md`.

## What the evidence supports

The late hosted match exceeded the 50 ms simulation budget, with routing spikes and queued worker operations. Socket buffers stayed empty in the sampled diagnostics. The first stability change prevents background AI routes from reserving the entire exact-planner arena ahead of human commands. That addresses a measured starvation mechanism; it does not remove all late-game simulation cost.

Disabling Armies did not speed up the matched local workload. Two synthetic Armies actually began moving sooner than 100 independently ordered squads. Keep shared Army routes and fix their lifecycle and spacing rather than removing Armies. Disabling construction yielded approximately 0.3% wall-time improvement in that fixture; build rejection command calls were cheap. Rebuilding into an enemy remains a real behavior defect, but those measurements do not establish it as the primary CPU bottleneck.

I cannot honestly promise every order starts within one second under any possible game state. CPU, entity growth, connection delay and browser work have finite capacity. The deliverable should be p95 below one second from input to visible movement in a defined supported workload, with p99 and worst-case reporting. Immediate command acknowledgement alone does not satisfy that target.

## Pass 1: progression and building controls — requests 1, 2, 3, 4 and 11

**Automatic tiers.** Make researched tier eligibility a domain rule shared by construction, capture and research completion. Upgrade the military and military production types when their associated technology completes. Use an explicit type-to-technology table rather than classifying by icon or recipe name. New buildings use the highest researched eligible tier. Existing completed buildings update through the normal lifecycle methods; unfinished buildings receive the eligible tier at completion. Captured buildings need an explicit rule for whether the captor retains their tier; my recommendation is to retain the physical tier and restrict production by the captor's research.

Do not heal damaged buildings for free during tier changes: preserve their health fraction and recalculate maximum integrity. Do not restart a paid production batch or charge an upgrade cost. Remove obsolete military upgrade actions from AI candidates and their resource reservations. Keep economic building upgrades only where they still have a distinct gameplay effect; request 11's broader removal needs an explicit type list before implementation.

**Factories.** `AutomaticProduction.ts` already chooses recipes by researched technology and compatible producer type, without an age check on the factory itself. Verify that manual production, tooltips and priorities share this rule, and cover Stone Age factories producing each later researched metal. This part may need consistency fixes rather than a new smelting mechanic.

**Delete.** Add an authoritative owned-building demolition command and a button on the left card. Recommended initial behavior: no refund, remove one selected building identity, cancel its unpaid future work, and apply normal cleanup for existing paid jobs, ports, leases, road/market indexes and wall endpoints. Bulk deletion and refunds are separate choices. The view model owns confirmation/selection; the simulation owns removal. Reject foreign, missing or newly captured identities at application time.

**Counts.** Project owned building counts by type once per state update into HUD badge values, including individual stack members. Avoid recounting the whole building array on every frame or reconstructing the HUD DOM. Show construction separately if it would otherwise mislead the player about completed capacity.

**Stack cap.** Change the shared construction limit from 10 to 15 and use it in authority, preview, AI placement and labels. Keep the existing trade cargo curve capped at level 10 unless explicitly changed: the prior trade design approved 3x cargo at 10. More buildings can still add production and fleet proportion without silently increasing merchant cargo beyond that balance.

Acceptance: research with damaged/unfinished/captured buildings; checkpoint round trip; production in old factories; demolition during production and port use; counts after capture/removal; allow stack 15 and reject 16 identically in preview and authority. Measure age-to-first-new-tier troop and idle equipment delays in matched seeds.

## Pass 2: bounded orders and river transport — requests 5, 6 and 7

**Selection.** The drag loop in `client/main.ts` adds every squad to `renderer.selected`; there is no 30-item truncation there. Internal movement cohorts are not a selection limit. Introduce one selection view model used by drag, Shift-add, control-group recall, Army selection and click selection. Drag chooses the highest troop counts with ID as deterministic tie-breaker, then keeps at most 30 squads. Decide whether Army controls are also limited to 30 or are an explicit separate control mechanism; the existing Army system supports larger capacities.

Add authoritative validation for loose-squad movement commands and have AI split requests into at most 30 members. Do not silently truncate an authoritative command, which would leave the player uncertain about which units received it. Chunking must retain one AI objective and leases instead of creating unrelated plans for every chunk.

**Transport.** Share a voyage plan for each order: start region, shore pair, water corridor and destination region. Queue cargo groups against that plan; reuse the validated corridor and find distinct landing slots locally. Tiny islands may use temporary friendly overlap as the explicitly allowed final landing fallback. Enemy bodies still block landing and movement. Do not search the full world separately for every squad or every boat. Cache by terrain/obstacle revision and relevant access rules, and invalidate safely when a coast is blocked or a port is captured.

For river peninsulas, compare bounded land-corridor cost against a shared water crossing. The destination point is intent, not a requirement that every squad fit an exact rigid rectangle centered on that tile. Report first embarkation, first movement, first landing and final delivery separately; a fast first boat can conceal a stalled remainder.

**Charge fallback.** `OrderGesture.ts` resolves the second click to a charge callback, while `main.ts` dispatches a charge command instead of the ordinary order. Put fallback in an authoritative intent handling path: if no valid charge is available, apply the ordinary attack/attack-move intent to the full selection. Do not issue two unrelated commands and retry based on a stale client eligibility check. A valid charge must still respect its costs and cooldowns. Cover mixed selections, cooldown, distance, stale/dead target and multiplayer outcomes.

Acceptance: 1/10/30-squad commands under late-game load; drag/Shift/group recall never exceed the limit; river peninsula and tiny-island crossings deliver the surviving roster; no enemy phasing; charge failures become useful ordinary orders. Target input-to-first-visible-motion p95 <1 s, and report all-member completion independently.

## Pass 3: formation steering and combat approaches — request 5

Friendly collision cannot simply be removed everywhere and expected to produce good combat. Keep enemy exclusion hard, use friendly separation as a soft local steering cost, and reserve controlled overlap for recovery after demonstrable lack of progress. Track blocked time and progress so fallback is local, temporary and reproducible.

Separate three responsibilities: a shared strategic corridor, local steering around actual bodies, and tactical attack-slot assignment. Allow formations to narrow/compress while travelling through gaps, then recover spacing on open ground. Collision should not repeatedly invalidate the strategic route because a friend moved into it. Tightening base spacing should use actual squad collision radii, not a global visual scale hack.

Attack orders should allocate reachable approach sectors around a target and distribute squads across a bounded set of nearby enemies. Limit attackers per sector, reuse reachable local corridors, and retarget only on target invalidation, blocked progress or a bounded refresh schedule. Preserve explicit Hold and bounded pursuit. Avoid 30 independent full searches to the same occupied target center. A defended gap must still stop enemy passage even before the fighting flag is set.

Frank Cheng's official [GDC presentation on Age of Empires IV](https://gdcvault.com/play/1027659/Pathing-in-Age-of-Empires) describes combining hierarchical A*, flow fields and steering for dynamic RTS movement. That supports separating shared global direction from local collision response. It does not establish that copying a complete flow-field architecture will improve this engine. The linked slide PDF was inaccessible during this review, so no undocumented AoE4 implementation details are assumed.

Start with cached corridor direction and bounded steering in the existing architecture. Prototype local destination flow fields only if matched profiles show per-squad connector work remains dominant. Measure field build cost, memory, invalidation rate and reuse before choosing a replacement. Do not rebuild a map-wide field on each order or each moving enemy.

Acceptance: opposing armies in a one-gap fixture, attacks from all sides, mixed melee/ranged approaches, friendly overtaking, tiny-island fallback and recovery after congestion. Record friendly overlap duration, idle-with-reachable-enemy time, enemy penetration violations, route work, steering time and input latency. Rendered visual review is required in addition to domain tests.

## Pass 4: island economy and trade — requests 8 and 9

Use cached connectivity/coast facts to detect island isolation and the absence of useful land targets. Shift marginal spending toward a usable port, goods-producing factory, city reserve growth, warship defense and one transport mission. Do not repeatedly buy land squads that have no destination or viable transport. Keep a local defense reserve and avoid naval purchases without a reachable water objective.

The current rules cap each faction at 48 traders, weight land/sea fleet allocation by factory/port stack members, and impose a 400-tick (20-second) site spawn cooldown. Factory trade also competes for available goods. A fleet target of 48 is not sufficient economic reasoning: 48 empty, paused or pathless merchants create work without income.

Plan toward 48 productive actors by measuring goods supply, permitted markets, route availability, successful deliveries and losses. Build enough production and safe sites to sustain the target; lower the active target when blockaded or without a useful destination. Reuse indexed market candidates and shared positive/negative route results. Prefer allies, then neutral destinations under the approved war-policy rules; keep sea foreign fallback and the ban on self sea trade.

Doubling rewards is a balance change, not a performance improvement. Current base price is 5 gold per good, with 1:2:4 self/allied/foreign ratios. Implementing the requested double means 10/20/40 land gold per good; sea uses its existing bounded distance modifier and minimum payout. Keep cargo, cooldown and distance curves unchanged for the first comparison so the effect can be measured. Track gold/minute, trade's share of income, first age transitions, military production and merchant captures. If it accelerates runaway winners, adjust the curve with evidence rather than hiding it in AI subsidies.

Acceptance: isolated factions invest in useful ports and actually sail; productive fleets approach the cap when safe; blockades reduce waste; foreign permissions and capture remain correct; matched seed progression and economy comparisons show the effect of doubled payouts.

## Pass 5: losing AI recovery — request 10 and the reported construction spam

Add a persistent recovery objective to the AI domain, driven by recent important-building losses and loss of a defensible core. Penalize recently captured sites and sites under hostile pressure. Use bounded retry/backoff keyed by site/type, with invalidation when territory and threat change. This should replace unproductive rebuild loops without prohibiting a genuine defended last stand.

Before funding a replacement military/economic core, assign reachable stragglers to defend it or attack the immediate invader. Respect existing combat and transport leases. If there is no viable core and there is reachable friendly uncaptured land across water, permit one bounded evacuation attempt, recording success/failure so it cannot become an infinite series of boats and searches. Territorial existence and elimination rules remain explicit; do not grant a defeated faction free land or buildings.

Reconcile Army ownership when an objective is released: disband or deliberately retain an idle Army, and reuse it before creating another. A stale Army is a lifecycle defect even if Army CPU is low. Preserve independent nearby squads when the Army objective is no longer useful.

Acceptance: captured-core pressure fixture; repeated site loss; scattered stragglers; safe/blocked evacuation; abandoned Army objective. Measure attempted/accepted builds, rapid recaptures, gold lost rebuilding, idle troops, objective count and bounded planning work. Do not claim CPU gains from fewer build commands without measuring site enumeration, world growth and routing too.

## Pass 6: hosted capacity and release qualification

Profile routing, movement and capture separately on the two-core ARM host, then test the production worker/publication pipeline with two actual clients. Add input timestamps through command admission, first authoritative displacement, publication and browser application. Record p50/p95/p99 and maximum, packet gaps, simulation speed and browser long tasks. A synthetic idle-human fixture alone cannot qualify a 40-minute human battle.

Keep one simultaneous Oracle match. Preserve offline skirmish. If serial tick cost remains above budget, first reduce duplicate HPA work, repeated obstacle queries and full-world capture scans with revisioned/local indexes. Larger hardware can buy headroom, but extra cores alone do not accelerate the serial simulation thread. Do not globally reduce tick frequency before measuring the gameplay and latency consequences.

Run a 60–90-minute supported-load soak after each material movement/AI pass, with scripted human commands, growing fleets, invasions, capture churn and disconnect/reconnect. Use short targeted fixtures during development and one broad release suite when a pass is ready. Gate deployment on unchanged canonical state across clients, no uncaught worker failures, bounded planner/queue age, acceptable simulation speed and measured visible player latency.

## Recommended order and promises

1. Progression/UI consistency and strict loose-selection limits.
2. Shared river transport and charge fallback.
3. Combat spacing, distributed targeting and temporary friendly recovery.
4. Island economy/trade and losing-AI recovery, measured independently.
5. Final ARM plus rendered-client soak and capacity review.

The promised deliverables are deterministic domain behavior, focused fixtures, reproducible before/after metrics, explicit remaining limitations and an idle-safe release. The sub-second target is a qualification target within the supported scenario, not an unconditional guarantee. This work keeps domain decisions authoritative and presentation state in view models; no DDD/MVVM departure is required.


## Implementation checkpoint — 1.2 pass one, 2026-10-04

Requests **1, 2 and 3** are implemented on branch `1.2`; see `2026-10-04-1.2-pass1.md` for validation and local playtesting. This includes automatic researched military tiers, research-gated factory smelting independent of physical tier, single-building human demolition with confirmation and no building refund, and individual building counts on the dock. The additional human-only unlimited gold custom rule is implemented for both hosted rooms and local skirmishes.

Requests **4–6** remain unimplemented until the user's first-pass local acceptance. Requests **7–11** remain subsequent work. The reviewed wider pass grouping does not override this narrower user-approved sequencing. The separate Oracle deployment contains only the preceding measured stability optimizations.
