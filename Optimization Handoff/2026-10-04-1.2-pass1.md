## Delivered scope

- Request 1: completed military and military-production buildings receive the highest researched eligible tier automatically. Ports use their naval unlock. Damage percentage, entity identity and paid production queues survive; upgrading adds neither gold/item cost nor a construction pause. Unfinished buildings receive eligibility when completed. Captured higher physical tiers remain; production still requires the current owner's research. City, factory, mine and oil infrastructure retain paid upgrades. Existing factories can smelt bronze, iron and steel as their recipes are researched, regardless of factory age. AI paid-upgrade candidates exclude automatically upgraded types.
- Request 2: the selected owned building card exposes Delete. A native modal asks **Are you sure?** and pins exactly one building identity. Confirm, Cancel, X and Escape behave explicitly; stale/captured intent is discarded. No building refund. Ordinary prepaid recruitment cancellation refunds only actual paid costs. Producer jobs/settings, attached walls/repairs, indexes and naval market facts are cleaned through domain lifecycle methods. The authoritative command rejects AI deletion, and no AI planner issues it.
- Request 3: each construction button shows individual owned structures, including stack members and construction. Ready/construction totals accompany the badge. Capture/removal updates counts; dynamic construction buttons receive only one badge.
- Extra test setting: **Infinite Gold for Players** in custom rooms and local skirmishes bypasses human gold costs. AI always uses finite balances, including a human seat under AI control. The rule follows human takeover/rejoin correctly. Stored balances, snapshots and hosted accounting remain finite; queued free purchases record zero refundable gold. Equipment, items, reserves, build/research times, caps and prerequisites remain normal. The treasury displays infinity.
