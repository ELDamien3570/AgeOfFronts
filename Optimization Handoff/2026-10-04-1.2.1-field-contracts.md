# Canonical ownership and replication ledger

Source inventory for the current 1.2.1 implementation. This records existing contracts and migration gaps; a documented owner is not proof that all external writes have been prohibited. `Expansion.snapshot()` is the projection boundary, not another canonical owner. The snapshot encoder's worker-boundary clone remains required wherever these projections contain live mutable aliases.

## Foundational mutation contracts

| Canonical family | Allowed runtime boundary | Dependents and restore contract |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Squads / ships | Skirmish add/update/remove; owned path installation where relinquishment is explicit | EntityCollection hooks maintain UnitIndex and entity journals. Position, owner, kind, alive and carrier transitions advance spatialRevision; cosmetic writes can reuse spatial geometry. Canonical array order remains the deterministic iteration order. Restore rebuilds facts and invalidates consumer journals. |
| Buildings | Skirmish building EntityCollection APIs | BuildingIndex, supply/producer and fortification lifecycle rules are separate synchronous domain responsibilities. Removing only the row is insufficient for ordinary deletion; applyCommand performs producer, fortification and recruitment cleanup. |
| Territory | Skirmish.changeOwner | Updates land/water owner membership, faction land totals, supply/operations territory dependencies, claim state and conservative footprint caches in defined synchronous order. Arrays remain publicly writable in some world ports; a typed effect/commit migration is still required. Tile journal records capture progression; do not infer ownership merely from draw state. |
| Boarding / shore transfer | Skirmish transport and ShoreTransport admission/commit | Carrier membership, cargo, reservations, route intent, receipt and squad presentation change together. Pending transfer states/cursors are behavior-relevant checkpoint data. Do not substitute a ship's connector for another actor without clearance/legal-passage validation. |
| Building upgrades / research | Expansion commands, Progression and Supply | Prevalidation, paid cost, building/unit definition and resulting revisions must be committed in existing order. They affect attack profiles, clearance, quotas, interception and route legality, beyond visible age labels. |
| Trade settlement | Trade's synchronous state machine plus Supply inventories and Player counters | Spawn total includes prizes; land/sea quotas exclude prizes only where the original rules do. Pass-local counts are rebuilt each step, with immediate corrections after prize transitions and original end-of-pass retirement timing. Delivery/capture/loss are exactly-once gameplay writes; checkpoint continuation must preserve state/cargo/wait/path admissions. |
| Checkpoint installation | Skirmish.restore | Known option/map/tile/identity errors reject before install. Deep invariant failure marks aggregate stopped; step, commands, checkpoints and further restore reject. The worker reports terminal recovery failure and terminates pending consumers. This is failure-stop, not an atomic rollback of every malformed subsystem payload. |

## Phase and memory boundaries

Combat and trade borrow spatial facts at a specific tick and geometry/membership revision. Ground/non-embarked, alive ground, all ships and alive warships are four separate inclusion contracts. Membership is never silently widened or narrowed to share one grid. Borrowed views are synchronous domain inputs; no background worker may retain them. Derived geometry may be reused across phases only while relevant revisions match.

Existing structural transport bounds are in `multiplayer/StateLimits.ts`: wire/payload/array/metadata token and byte limits, encoder slots and the eight-state / 16 MiB client queue are distinct envelopes. V8 old-generation limits do not bound RSS or external arrays. They do **not** yet provide a complete lifetime retained-byte limit for every delta family. Before extending additional family deltas, specify producer/decoder retained identities, geometry bytes, tombstone age, overflow baseline size and failure behavior on the same profile. Do not introduce arbitrary gameplay population caps to make a decoder benchmark pass.

Latest-change journals bound retained distinct IDs and expose overflow floors; they do not retain one record per historical mutation. Their internal linked order permits suffix reads proportional to changed retained IDs. Added and removed revision semantics, ID reuse ordering, independent consumers and reset fallbacks remain tested against the original scan oracle. New families must use their real mutation owner, not an unbounded per-tick diff cache.
